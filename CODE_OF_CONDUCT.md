# Code of Conduct

SlideRelay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and focus on what's
best for the people using SlideRelay. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or
issue (the **⋯** menu), or email the maintainer at **hello@manishkandel.com.np**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove comments, lock discussions, or block people who
break this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
